Services

Six areas where most platform problems actually live. Pick one, or start with an assessment and let the priorities come from evidence.

01

Cloud Infrastructure

Environments that are predictable to build, cheap to run and safe to change. We start from your actual requirements rather than a reference architecture that works for everyone except you.

  • Landing zones, account structure and network segmentation
  • Kubernetes cluster design, build and upgrade lifecycle
  • Storage, backup, retention and tested recovery
  • Cost review and rightsizing

Typical outcomes

  • Repeatable environment creation instead of hand-built ones
  • Documented, rehearsed recovery with known RTO/RPO
  • Reduced spend without service degradation

02

CI/CD & Automation

Pipelines where a change reaching production is the expected outcome rather than an event. Fast feedback, small changes, and a rollback that anyone on the team can perform.

  • Pipeline architecture, gates and quality checks
  • Infrastructure as Code modules and review standards
  • Release strategies: blue/green, canary, progressive
  • Reusable pipelines and self-service templates

Typical outcomes

  • Lead time from commit to production measured and falling
  • Deploys that are boring and reversible
  • Audit trail for every change automatically generated

03

Security & Compliance

Controls that live in the delivery path, so compliance evidence is a by-product of shipping software rather than a separate project nobody has time for.

  • Container image and dependency scanning
  • Secrets management and rotation strategy
  • Policy as code and admission controls
  • Evidence collection for ISO 27001, SOC 2 and Cyber Essentials

Typical outcomes

  • Findings fixed at source instead of triaged repeatedly
  • Continuous evidence, not an annual scramble
  • Clear, defensible answer to "how do you know?"

04

Observability

Instrumentation that tells you what users experienced, not just that a container exists. Designed around questions your on-call engineer needs answered at 3am.

  • Metrics, logs and distributed tracing
  • Alerting tuned to be actionable rather than noisy
  • Service-level objectives and error budgets
  • Cost and capacity visibility

Typical outcomes

  • Fewer pages, and the pages that remain matter
  • Incidents diagnosed from telemetry, not guesswork
  • Reliability targets agreed and actually measured

05

Platform Engineering

Removing the ticket queue. Internal platforms exist so product teams can ship without waiting on infrastructure, and so infrastructure work is done once rather than per team.

  • Developer portals and self-service workflows
  • Service templates, scaffolding and golden paths
  • Platform SLAs and support model definition
  • Documentation treated as a product

Typical outcomes

  • New services stood up in hours, not sprints
  • Platform work centralised and de-duplicated
  • Developers shipping without deep infra knowledge

06

Consulting & Audit

An independent read on what you have now, with a plan you can execute in whatever capacity you actually have. We are explicit when the answer is "you don't need this yet".

  • Platform, pipeline and security reviews
  • Reliability and readiness assessments
  • Technical due diligence for investors and acquisitions
  • Architecture and tooling recommendations

Typical outcomes

  • Written findings with severity and effort estimates
  • Prioritised roadmap separated into now/next/later
  • No dependency on us to keep executing it

Not sure where to start?

A short assessment gives you an independent, prioritised view of your platform. No obligation, and you keep the findings either way.
